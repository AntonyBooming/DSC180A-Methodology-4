**What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?**

The most interesting topic covered in my domain is PCA. I like how it takes the activity of hundreds of neurons and reduces it to a few components that still capture most of the variation in the data. It was also interesting to compare it to nonlinear methods like tSNE and UMAP, which changes depending on parameters.

**Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.**

I would like to see how well PCA does in terms of representing the data as accurately as possible. Using the Allen Neuropixels data, I could compare PCA to UMAP by measuring how well stimulus information, like grating orientation, can be decoded from each low-dimensional representation.
